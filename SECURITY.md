# Security

## Reporting a problem

Email VENIN's Security Officer at `security@venin.space`. Include what you did, what happened, and the commit or version you used.

You can also report privately through GitHub: open this repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Do not open a public issue or put details in a pull request title.

## What happens next

We acknowledge your report, look into it and tell you what we decide. If a fix is needed, we tell you when it is released.
